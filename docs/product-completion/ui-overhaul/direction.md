# Art Direction

## Design Read

Neutral multi-vendor marketplace for buyers and marketplace operators. Precision-retail language with product-led public pages and exact operational workspaces.

## Dials

| Dial | Value | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Design variance | 6 | Offset compositions without experimental navigation |
| Motion intensity | 4 | Restrained reveals and tactile feedback |
| Visual density | 6 | Efficient controls with deliberate whitespace |

## Emotional Register

**Precision retail:** calm, exact, capable.

- Cool neutral surfaces, graphite text, cobalt accent.
- Bold sans display type with compact supporting copy.
- Crisp product crops and controlled image ratios.
- Sharp hierarchy, sparse decoration, no craft-market nostalgia.
- Public pages feel premium. Workspaces feel systematic.

## Image Language

- Studio-first product photography on cool-gray surfaces.
- Contextual lifestyle images limited to hero and store storytelling.
- Consistent lighting, crop, and color treatment.
- No rustic scenes, generic stock lifestyle, or fake UI screenshots.

## Voice

- Marketplace language first. Demo language only on `/demo`, demo login helpers, and operational disclosure.
- Concrete verbs and short labels.
- No inflated claims, poetic filler, or fake metrics.
